# 📊 Superstore Sales & Profitability Analytics

### End-to-End Data Analytics Project | Python | SQL Server | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Superstore sales data to identify business trends, evaluate profitability, understand customer purchasing behavior, and uncover opportunities for business growth.

Using Python, SQL Server, and Power BI, I performed data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, business-focused SQL queries, and interactive dashboard development to convert raw sales data into actionable insights.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability performance.
- Identify top-performing and loss-making products.
- Evaluate sales trends across product categories and regions.
- Understand customer segments and purchasing patterns.
- Analyze the impact of discounts on profitability.
- Identify opportunities to improve revenue and profit margins.

## 🔍 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning & Exploratory Data Analysis (Python)

- Cleaned and prepared 9,000+ sales records using Pandas and NumPy.
- Checked missing values, duplicate records, and data consistency.
- Performed exploratory data analysis to understand sales and profit patterns.
- Analyzed performance across product categories, customer segments, and geographical regions.
- Used Matplotlib for data visualization.

### 2. SQL Analysis (SQL Server)

Used SQL Server to perform business-oriented analysis, including:

- Total sales, profit, and quantity analysis.
- Category and sub-category profitability analysis.
- Top-performing and loss-making product identification.
- Regional and customer segment performance.
- Discount impact on profitability.
- Ranking products and customers based on business performance.

### 3. Interactive Dashboard (Power BI)

Developed an interactive dashboard to monitor key business metrics and identify performance trends.

Key metrics and analysis include:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity Sold
- Profit Margin
- Sales by Category and Sub-Category
- Regional Sales Performance
- Customer Segment Analysis
- Discount vs. Profitability Analysis

## 💡 Key Business Insights

The analysis focuses on identifying the following business opportunities:

1. **Category Performance:** Compare sales and profitability across product categories to identify high-revenue and low-margin segments.

2. **Discount Optimization:** Evaluate how different discount levels affect profit margins and identify potential profit leakage.

3. **Regional Performance:** Identify high-performing and underperforming regions to support better business decisions.

4. **Product Analysis:** Identify products generating high revenue as well as products contributing to losses.

5. **Customer Segmentation:** Analyze customer purchasing behavior to identify valuable customer segments.

## 📊 Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive view of sales, profit, products, customers, and regional performance.

## 📈 Business Recommendations

- Review discount strategies for products with low or negative profitability.
- Focus on improving margins in high-sales but low-profit categories.
- Identify underperforming products and evaluate pricing strategies.
- Strengthen sales strategies in high-performing regions.
- Use customer segmentation to improve targeted marketing efforts.
